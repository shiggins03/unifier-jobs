About the Position

We’re seeking a 
Senior Document Control Specialist 
to support large, complex heavy civil infrastructure programs, including highways, roads, bridges, transit (bus and rail), aviation (international airports), and water/wastewater projects delivered through both traditional and alternative delivery methods. This position is the technical lead for document control on a major rail capital program, serving as the subject matter expert for the enterprise document control system and the standards behind it. You’ll keep controlled record sets accurate, traceable, and audit-ready while supporting system rollout, adoption, and continuous improvement across projects and functional groups.

This is a long-term, full-time, hybrid (3-4 days per week on-site) position based in Sacramento, CA.

Responsibilities
 
may include, but are not limited to, the following:

Serve as the technical subject matter expert for document control systems, processes, and standards, and provide guidance and mentoring to document controllers, program analysts, and project teams.

Oversee document intake, processing, distribution, storage, and archival for design, construction, right-of-way, and environmental record sets across a rail capital program.

Review document submittals for completeness, accuracy, metadata, naming convention, version control, and classification compliance before acceptance into the document management system.

Perform system administration for the enterprise document control platform, including configuration, permissions, workflows, and troubleshooting.

Support deployment, configuration, and optimization of the enterprise document control system, and participate in defining system requirements, workflows, user roles, and reporting capabilities.

Evaluate system gaps, risks, and improvement opportunities, and recommend enhancements that improve efficiency and data integrity.
