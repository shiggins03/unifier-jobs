Position Brand Description:
The Global Facilities Delivery (GFD) Organization, part of Corporate Engineering, delivers new and renovated capital assets around the world.  Our mission is accomplished through a highly leveraged organization of design, construction and qualification contractors.
Many of the assets delivered focus on manufacturing medical innovations and delivering innovative solutions to patients.  Some of the assets delivered support an expansion in supply of existing medicines. 
Role Summary:
The Capital Project Cost Estimator develops owner-side Class 5 through Class 1 estimates 
across the full range of construction disciplines supporting complex pharmaceutical manufacturing and infrastructure projects. Scopes include civil, structural, and architectural (CSA) work such as site development, building envelope, and demolition; mechanical, electrical, plumbing, and fire protection (MEP) systems, building controls, and utility distribution; and industrial process scopes such as major process equipment, packaged systems, process piping, hygienic systems, and process utilities. The role converts evolving engineering scope, design information, equipment and vendor data, execution strategies, market data, and benchmarks into complete, traceable, and decision-ready estimates.
