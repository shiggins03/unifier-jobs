About the Positions

We’re seeking 
Contract Administrators
 to support large, complex heavy civil infrastructure programs, including highways, roads, bridges, transit (bus and rail), aviation (international airports), and water/wastewater projects delivered through both traditional and alternative delivery methods. These roles carry contracts and task orders through the full life cycle, from solicitation and proposal through award, administration, modification, and closeout, on transit and transportation capital programs. We are looking for people who know how to build a clean solicitation, who can work across a range of contract and agreement types, and who keep contract files defensible from the first draft to final payment.

This post is for future roles and not for a specific opportunity that is available right now. We accept applications and conduct interviews in advance of upcoming hiring needs. When a candidate is selected, their profile is activated in our talent pool for this specialty area. We notify our talent pool candidates first whenever we have a project need.

Responsibilities
 
may include, but are not limited to, the following:

Support each assigned contract and task order through its full life cycle, from solicitation and proposal through award, administration, modification, and closeout, on transit and transportation capital programs.

Develop and review solicitation packages, including requests for proposals, invitations for bid, requests for quotes, scopes of work, specifications, and evaluation criteria, for completeness and conformance with program and agency procurement requirements.

Coordinate pre-proposal and pre-bid meetings, respond to bidder and proposer questions, and prepare addenda and amendments before proposals are due.

Support proposal and bid evaluation, including coordinating selection committees, documenting evaluation results, and preparing award recommendations and approval packages for executive concurrence.
