About the Position

We’re seeking a 
Contract Administrator
 to support large, complex heavy civil infrastructure programs, including highways, roads, and transit projects delivered through both traditional and alternative delivery methods. You’ll administer multiple task order contracts across their full life cycle, from solicitation and proposal evaluation through award, modifications, and close-out, keeping every task order compliant, well documented, and within its authorized scope and value.

This is a long-term, full-time, on-site position located in the greater DMV Area.

Responsibilities
 
may include, but are not limited to, the following:

Administer multiple task order contracts at the same time, managing each through the full life cycle, from task order request and proposal through award, performance, modification, and close-out.

Develop solicitation packages, including requests for proposals, requests for quotes, and bid documents, in coordination with project managers and technical staff.

Review statements of work, specifications, and scope documents for clarity, completeness, and consistency with contract terms and conditions.

Evaluate task order proposals and price quotes for responsiveness, scope alignment, labor categories, rates, and level of effort, and document recommendations for award.

Support cost and price analysis, comparisons against independent cost estimates, and negotiations, and record the results in the contract file.

Prepare and process contract modifications, task order amendments, and change documentation, and track each through review and approval.

Monitor contract ceilings, funding, periods of performance, and invoicing against task order terms, and flag issues early.

Maintain complete, audit-ready contract files across multiple contract and agreement types, including deliverables, insurance, bonding, and compliance records.
