About the Positions

We’re seeking 
Construction Inspectors
 of various skill levels across civil and structural, mechanical and plumbing, and electrical disciplines to support large, heavy-civil infrastructure programs including transit (both rail and bus) and transportation projects delivered through both traditional and alternative methods. Your focus will be field verification to confirm that installed work matches approved plans and specifications, document what happens on site every day, and catch problems early on.

This post is for future roles and not for a specific opportunity that is available right now. We accept applications and conduct interviews in advance of upcoming hiring needs. When a candidate is selected, their profile is activated in our talent pool for this specialty area. 
We notify our talent pool candidates first whenever we have a project need.

Responsibilities
 
may include, but are not limited to, the following:

Inspect construction work for conformance with approved plans, specifications, permits, codes, and quality requirements.

Document daily construction activity, including labor, equipment, materials, quantities, weather, and progress photographs.

Verify materials on delivery against submittals, certifications, and approved product data, and reject nonconforming material.

Coordinate and witness field testing, and track test results, deficiencies, and corrective actions to closure.

Identify and report nonconforming work, safety concerns, and design conflicts to the resident engineer promptly.

Measure and record pay quantities supporting contractor progress payments.

Coordinate with contractors, utility owners, agency staff, and third parties during active construction operations.

Support survey verification, layout checks, and as-built documentation.

Enforce traffic control, work zone, and site safety requirements within the assigned work area.

Support punch list development, testing and startup, and closeout documentation
