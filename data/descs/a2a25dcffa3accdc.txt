About the Position

We’re seeking an 
Assistant Project Manager
 to support large, complex heavy civil infrastructure programs, including highways, roads, and transit projects delivered through both traditional and alternative delivery methods. You’ll support assigned task orders from planning and design through construction and closeout, keeping scope, cost, and schedule on track and giving project leadership the information needed to make sound, well-documented decisions.

This is a long-term, full-time, on-site position located in the greater DMV Area.

Responsibilities
 
may include, but are not limited to, the following:

Support multiple assigned task orders through all project phases, including planning, design, procurement, construction, and closeout.

Coordinate development of task order scope, schedule, and budget, and track performance against approved baselines.

Maintain project controls data, including cost, schedule, and forecast reporting, and flag variances with recommended corrective actions.

Review design submittals and deliverables at each milestone for conformance with project criteria, and track comment resolution to closure.

Support procurement activities, including scope development, review of bid documents, and evaluation of proposals.

Oversee construction-phase progress alongside field staff, verifying installed work and submitted quantities against approved documents.

Evaluate change requests, requests for information, and potential claims for technical merit, cost, and schedule impact, and prepare recommendations.

Coordinate with internal departments, operations groups, third parties, and utility owners to resolve issues and minimize service impacts.

Prepare progress reports, meeting minutes, and presentation materials, and maintain complete project documentation through closeout.

Attributes

Excellent written and verbal communication and interpersonal skills.

Excellent multi-tasking and organizational skills.

Collaborative minds
