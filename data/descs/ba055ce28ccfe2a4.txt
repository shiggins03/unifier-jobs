STV is seeking a Cost Engineering Manager to join our Project Controls department at our New York City office.
 This position will support major transportation, transit, infrastructure, aviation, and capital improvement programs by providing cost management, forecasting, budgeting, earned value analysis, and project performance reporting services throughout the project lifecycle.
The Cost Engineering Specialist II will work closely with project managers, schedulers, estimators, construction managers, and client stakeholders to ensure effective cost control and project financial performance. The ideal candidate will possess strong analytical skills, experience evaluating complex project cost data, and a solid understanding of project controls principles and best practices. This role offers the opportunity to contribute to some of the region's most significant infrastructure programs while advancing a rewarding career within STV's growing Project Controls practice.
Essential Duties & Responsibilities
Cost Management & Forecasting
Develop, maintain, and monitor project budgets, cost reports, forecasts, and cash flow projections.
Track project expenditures, commitments, accruals, and budget utilization.
Perform cost variance analysis and identify emerging budget risks and opportunities.
Support Estimate at Completion (EAC) development and forecasting activities.
Analyze project financial performance against approved budgets and established baselines.
Review invoices, contractor payment applications, and supporting documentation for accuracy and compliance.
Earned Value & Performance Management
Support earned value management (EVM) reporting and project performance analysis.
Monitor key performance indicators and provide recommendations for corrective actions.
Assist project teams in evaluating cost trends and productivity impacts.
