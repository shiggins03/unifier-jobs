About the Positions

We’re seeking 
Office Engineers
 of various skill levels to support large, complex heavy civil infrastructure programs, including highways, roads, bridges, transit (bus and rail), aviation (international airports), and water/wastewater projects delivered through both traditional and alternative delivery methods. These positions run the construction office: submittals, RFIs, contract records, pay applications, and change documentation. You’ll keep that record complete and current so decisions on payment, time, and entitlement hold up months later under audit or claim review.

This post is for future roles and not for a specific opportunity that is available right now. We accept applications and conduct interviews in advance of upcoming hiring needs. When a candidate is selected, their profile is activated in our talent pool for this specialty area. 
We notify our talent pool candidates first whenever we have a project need.
