About the Positions

We’re seeking 
Project Coordinators
 to support large, complex heavy civil infrastructure programs, including highways, roads, bridges, transit (bus and rail), aviation (international airports), and water/wastewater projects delivered through both traditional and alternative delivery methods. These positions keep the project office running: submittals and requests for information moving inside contractual time frames, action items closed, cost and progress reporting current, and a project record that holds up long after the people who built it have moved on. We place coordinators into program offices, field offices, and construction management teams, so we are looking for people who have carried the day to day coordination load on a program of real size.

This post is for future roles and not for a specific opportunity that is available right now. We accept applications and conduct interviews in advance of upcoming hiring needs. When a candidate is selected, their profile is activated in our talent pool for this specialty area. We notify our talent pool candidates first whenever we have a project need.

Responsibilities
 
may include, but are not limited to, the following:

Coordinate the daily operations of the project office, including meeting agendas, minutes, and action item tracking, and drive open items to closure with the parties who own them.

Track submittals, requests for information, and change documentation through the program’s management information system, and keep contractor and reviewer turnaround inside the contractual time frames.

Maintain the project record, including correspondence, transmittals, contract documents, and supporting files, so decisions stay traceable through audit, claim review, and funding review.

Review contractor and consultant deliverables for completeness and conformance with the contract’s documentation requirements before they reach the program’s technical reviewers.
