About the Position

We’re seeking a 
Project Coordinator
 to support large, complex heavy civil infrastructure programs, including highways, roads, and transit projects delivered through both traditional and alternative delivery methods. This position supports program and construction management teams on a major transit program, keeping records, plans, specifications, and related documents organized, current, and easy to retrieve across multiple concurrent task orders. Your work keeps teams working from the right information and keeps the administrative side of the program accurate and on time.

This is a long-term, full-time, on-site position located in the greater DMV area.

Responsibilities
 
may include, but are not limited to, the following:

Maintain project records, plans, specifications, and related documents in accordance with program document control procedures.

Coordinate administrative activities across multiple concurrent task orders, tracking deliverables, deadlines, and status.

Track submittals, transmittals, requests for information, and correspondence through review and response, and keep logs current.

Verify document versions and distribution so teams work from current plans and specifications.

Support task order administration by tracking authorizations, budgets, hours, and invoice backup documentation.

Prepare routine correspondence, memoranda, reports, and presentations, checking each for accuracy in grammar, spelling, punctuation, and figures.

Prepare meeting agendas, record minutes, and track action items to closure.

Maintain filing systems and records retention in line with program and agency requirements, and support audits and document requests.

Coordinate schedules and meetings among program staff, agency stakeholders, and consultants.

Attributes

Excellent written and verbal communication and interpersonal skills, including thorough knowledge of business English, spelling, punctuation, and arithmetic.
