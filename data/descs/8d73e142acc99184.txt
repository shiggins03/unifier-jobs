About the Position

We’re seeking a 
Construction Manager
 to support a large, complex, heavy-civil rail infrastructure program. This role will be responsible for strategically directing and overseeing construction-related activities, ensuring alignment from pre-construction through delivery.

This is a long-term, full-time, on-site position located in the greater San Francisco Bay Area, CA.

Responsibilities
 
may include, but are not limited to, the following:

Own the constructability strategy, identifying construction-related challenges and resolutions early in the process and continually throughout all phases.

Serve as the accountable lead for construction input to design activities and milestones.

Lead construction oversight across multiple contracts using a mix of delivery methods, spanning early works, specialty and heavy civil construction, systems installation, and facility fit-out phases.

Lead, oversee, and coordinate construction management services.

Ensure the people, processes, and verification protocols are in place so that the built project is consistent with contractual requirements.

Lead and develop direct reports across construction contract management teams, setting expectations, allocating resources, and building bench strength as the program scales.

Coordinate with engineering, project controls, procurement, right of way, safety, and quality teams to align construction sequencing with program schedule, budget, and risk priorities.

Support federal, state, regional, and local oversight requirements, including third-party project management oversight reviews and reporting.

Elevate construction risks, trends, and mitigation strategies to program leadership, and support the resolution of changes, claims, and disputes.

Attributes

Excellent written and verbal communication and interpersonal skills.

Excellent multi-tasking and organizational skills.

Collaborative mindset that fosters teamwork, trust, and positive relationships.
