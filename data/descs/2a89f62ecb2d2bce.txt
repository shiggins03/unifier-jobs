Job Description
Overview
AtkinsRéalis is seeking a Project Coordinator to join our Aviation & Ports Program Management/Construction Management Team. This is an 
entry-level
 position and is based out of Los Angeles, CA or San Diego, CA.  
Your Role
Support aviation program and construction management teams with day-to-day project controls, coordination, documentation, and reporting activities for airport capital projects.
Assist with tracking project schedules, budgets, action items, meeting notes, submittals, RFIs, change items, and other project information using established tools and templates.
Help prepare project status reports, dashboards, presentation materials, meeting agendas, and follow-up summaries for internal teams, clients, and project stakeholders.
Participate in coordination with project managers, construction managers, estimators, schedulers, designers, contractors, and airport stakeholders to support successful project delivery.
Assist with document control activities, including organizing project files, maintaining logs, reviewing basic project records, and helping ensure information is accurate and up to date.
Support site or field-related activities, when appropriate, by observing construction progress, documenting field notes, and helping prepare follow-up items under the direction of experienced professionals.
Contribute to research, data collection, and analysis related to airport facilities, infrastructure programs, construction trends, project delivery, and client needs.
Perform other duties as assigned to support the Aviation & Ports PMCM team and provide exposure to the full lifecycle of airport infrastructure delivery.
