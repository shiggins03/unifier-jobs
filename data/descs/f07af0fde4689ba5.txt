About the Position

We’re seeking a 
PMIS Configuration Consultant
 to configure, implement, and support project management information systems on large-scale, heavy-civil infrastructure programs across highways, roads and bridges, transit (rail and bus), aviation, and water sectors. You’ll build out PMIS modules for budget, cost, schedule, risk, and reporting, and partner with project controls teams to keep program data structured, accurate, and audit ready.

This is a long-term, full-time, hybrid position located in Oakland, CA, that will require up to 50% travel.

Responsibilities
 
may include, but are not limited to, the following:

Configure PMIS processes for budget, cost, change orders, payments, risk, issues, cash flow, and earned value to support project controls, project management, and program management functions.

Configure security roles, user access, and integrations between PMIS platforms, scheduling systems, document management systems, and ERP systems.

Configure cash flow, earned value, and other modules within the PMIS solution.

Identify and correct inconsistencies in data structures, coding, and hierarchy across project controls systems.

Develop and maintain data models that support integrated reporting across cost, schedule, and performance metrics, and drive continuous improvement of data structures, reporting processes, and data quality across the program.

Develop project reports and dashboards aligned with client and program reporting requirements.

Automate data extraction, transformation, and reconciliation across PMIS and integrated systems using SQL and/or Python.

Reconcile and validate data across multiple reporting systems to maintain accuracy and consistency.

Collaborate with project controls managers, cost engineers, and schedulers to ensure data structures support program controls requirements.

Support end-user training and knowledge transfer on deployed PMIS configurations, workflows, and reporting tools.
