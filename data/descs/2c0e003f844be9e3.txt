About the Position

We’re seeking an experienced 
Railroad/Roadway Bridge and Guideway Engineer
 to support large, heavy-civil infrastructure programs, including highways, roads, bridges, transit (both rail and bus), aviation, and water/wastewater projects delivered through both traditional and alternative methods. This role focuses on infrastructure maintenance inspection and quality assurance: verifying that the physical plant conforms to approved plans, specifications, codes, and safety standards, documenting conditions, identifying non-conforming elements, and coordinating corrective action with project engineers, contractors, and construction management staff.

This is a long-term, full-time, on-site position located in Fresno, CA.

Responsibilities
 
may include, but are not limited to, the following:

Perform daily inspections of bridges, guideways, elevated structures, retaining walls, foundations, and related civil infrastructure to verify compliance with approved plans, specifications, and contract requirements.

Monitor structural elements, including structural concrete, reinforcing steel, prestressed and post-tensioned systems, structural steel, bearings, expansion joints, precast elements, falsework, and shoring.

Verify that built elements conform to project quality requirements and applicable industry standards.

Review or develop inspection and testing reports, material certifications, placement records, and quality control documentation for accuracy and completeness.

Document inspection findings, deficiencies, field observations, photographs, and project progress using electronic and other reporting systems.

Identify non-conforming elements and coordinate corrective actions with contractors, resident engineers, quality assurance staff, and project management.

Witness and document field testing, material sampling, structural load testing, and acceptance testing across the program’s physical plant.
