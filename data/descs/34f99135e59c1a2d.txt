Position Brand Description:
The Global Facilities Delivery (GFD) Organization, part of Corporate Engineering, delivers new and renovated capital assets around the world.  Our mission is accomplished through a highly leveraged organization of design, construction and qualification contractors.
Many of the assets delivered focus on manufacturing medical innovations and delivering innovative solutions to patients.  Some of the assets delivered support an expansion in supply of existing medicines. 
Role Summary:
The Industrial Process Capital Cost Estimator develops owner-side Class 5 through Class 1 estimates for major pharmaceutical process equipment, packaged systems, process piping, hygienic systems, process utilities, equipment installation, and related construction scopes. The role converts evolving process and engineering scope, equipment data, vendor information, execution strategies, market data, and benchmarks into complete, traceable, and decision-ready estimates. The role will assess equipment freight, rigging, setting, modularization, fabrication, specialty welding, testing, passivation, validation support, spare parts, and vendor field services.
