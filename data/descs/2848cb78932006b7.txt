Oracle Cloud Project Portfolio Management Delivery Architect

JOB SUMMARY:

A Delivery Architect at Argano is a highly experienced professional in the consulting family responsible for providing solution leadership to mid to large size client engagements and contributing to the growth and development of the consulting practice. The ideal candidate will be a consummate professional who can thrive in an upbeat and innovative environment and collaborate with clients to deliver a state-of-the-art solution. The individual will excel at delivering high quality service and cutting-edge solutions to Argano clients in the Oracle community. The Oracle Cloud Delivery Architect will focus on guiding and assisting clients through all phases of Oracle ERP Cloud implementation. The Oracle Cloud Delivery Architect is expected to have expertise in implementing a wide range of Oracle Cloud applications. The successful candidate will have completed five successful Oracle Cloud implementations. An Oracle Cloud Delivery Architect is responsible for the overall Enterprise Cloud solution. The Delivery Architect will lead the implementation team in all phases of the implementation. This individual will identify and apply creative and innovative approaches to resolving product implementation and integration obstacles. They provide expert advice, guidance, and solutions related to technology to businesses and organizations. Their goal is to help clients optimize their technology infrastructure, processes, and strategies to achieve their business objectives more efficiently and effectively. 

RESPONSIBILITIES:

Lead mid to large size and complex consulting projects simultaneously.

Extensive whole lifecycle experience in leading Oracle SaaS requirements workshops, solutions architecture & design discussions

Using in-depth knowledge of Oracle Cloud Applications, as well as industry best practice expertise, consults with clients to provide cost effective solutions to client business scenarios.
