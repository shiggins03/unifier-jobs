About the Position

We’re seeking a 
Geotechnical Field Engineer
 to support large, complex heavy civil infrastructure programs, including highways, roads, bridges, transit (bus and rail), aviation (international airports), and water/wastewater projects delivered through both traditional and alternative delivery methods. In this role, you will support field investigations, engineering analysis, calculations, and recommendations; the design and development of plans and specifications; and the writing, preparation, and review of related reports for the services provided.

This is a long-term, full-time, on-site position located in Atlanta, GA.

Responsibilities
 
may include, but are not limited to, the following:

Operating and maintaining geotechnical testing equipment.

Classifying soil, rock, and geological samples obtained from drilling operations.

Assisting in developing the scope and budget of work for routine projects.

Analyzing data, performing engineering calculations and analysis, and reporting data to clients under the direction of a Professional Engineer.

Interacting with the client and project team.

Following safety rules, guidelines, and standards for all projects.

Natural curiosity, problem-solving abilities, and a passion for continuous improvement.
