Director of Construction Technologies
The Director of Construction Technologies is a strategic leader responsible for overseeing the delivery, implementation, integration, and optimization of construction technology solutions for large, complex capital projects. This role leads a team of technology consultants, implementation specialists, developers, and trainers who support project owners, contractors, and construction management firms throughout the project lifecycle.
As a platform-agnostic technology leader, the Director of Construction Technologies serves as a trusted advisor to clients, helping them evaluate, deploy, configure, customize, and maximize the value of industry-leading construction software solutions. The Director ensures successful adoption of digital construction technologies while driving operational excellence, innovation, and measurable project outcomes.
