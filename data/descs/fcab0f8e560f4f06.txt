The Global Facilities Delivery (GFD) Organization, part of Manufacturing & Quality, delivers new and renovated capital assets around the world. Our mission is accomplished through a highly leveraged organization of project management, engineering, construction, and qualification contractors. Many of the assets delivered focus on medical innovation and delivering innovative solutions to patients. Some of the assets delivered will expand the supply of existing medicines or strengthen the supply chain.
The Advisor/Sr Advisor of Program Governance and Operations is embedded within a single large-scale capital program and serves as the strategic integrator and trusted advisor to the program's senior leader. The role exists to extend leadership capacity: it converts leadership intent into clear operating norms, execution plans, and communications, and it protects leadership focus by resolving friction before it requires escalation.
The role is accountable for the program's leadership operating rhythm, executive communications, program office operations, and organizational health. It works alongside the Program PMO Lead, who owns the program's PMO framework and delivery governance; this position operates the leadership forum layer that sits above that framework and ensures decisions are made when they need to be made.
