Position Brand Description
The Global Facilities Delivery (GFD) Organization, part of Corporate Engineering, delivers new and renovated capital assets around the world.  Our mission is accomplished through a highly leveraged organization of design, construction and qualification contractors.
Many of the assets delivered focus on manufacturing of medical innovations and delivering innovative solutions to patients.  Some of the assets delivered support an expansion in supply of existing medicines.
Role Summary
The Advisor, Project Controls Engineer is the central steward for project controls functions within Global Facilities Delivery (GFD). This role ensures best-in-class practices in budgeting, cost control, earned value management, change and risk management, forecasting, and planning. The Project Controls Engineer provides strategic governance, leadership, and mentorship to project controls teams across a diverse portfolio of capital projects, with a strong focus on cost management and business value. The incumbent drives continuous improvement, compliance, and capability development, enabling successful project delivery and organizational growth.
