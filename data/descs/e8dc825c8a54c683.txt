About the Position

We’re seeking a 
Document Control Specialist
 to support large, complex heavy civil infrastructure programs, including highways, roads, bridges, transit (bus and rail), aviation (international airports), and water/wastewater projects delivered through both traditional and alternative delivery methods. This position supports the document control function on a major rail capital program, keeping the electronic document management system accurate, traceable, and audit-ready as submittals, drawings, and correspondence move through review. You’ll be the person who knows where a record lives, which revision governs, and whether the approval trail holds up under audit.

This is a long-term, full-time, hybrid (3-4 days per week on-site) position based in Sacramento, CA.

Responsibilities
 
may include, but are not limited to, the following:

Maintain document control systems and workflows for document submissions, approvals, and distribution.

Support setup and configuration of the electronic document management system, including folder architecture, metadata schemas, naming conventions, automated workflows, and user roles and permissions.

Receive and log incoming submittals, drawings, and correspondence into the document control system, verifying that metadata including title, number, revision, discipline, and originator is accurate and complete.

Maintain master copies of controlled documents, track revisions, and verify that formatting and naming conventions conform to program standards.

Coordinate with contractors, consultants, and program staff so required documentation is submitted on schedule and retrievable when needed.

Verify compliance with document control standards, QA/QC protocols, and contractual, legal, and regulatory requirements, including ISO 9001:2015, FTA, and FRA.

Perform periodic audits for metadata accuracy, overdue reviews, unauthorized revisions, and approval gaps, and flag inconsistencies for corrective action.
