About the Positions

We’re seeking 
Senior Right-of-Way Engineers
 to support large, complex heavy civil infrastructure programs, including highways, roads, bridges, transit (bus and rail), aviation (international airports), and water/wastewater projects delivered through both traditional and alternative delivery methods. Your focus is the right-of-way engineering that keeps corridor delivery moving: right-of-way plans, legal descriptions, parcel and easement documentation, and the coordination across design, survey, acquisition, and construction that decides whether property is available when crews need it. You’ll review and approve those deliverables and establish a property rights position that holds up when schedule and funding are on the line.

This post is for future roles and not for a specific opportunity that is available right now. We accept applications and conduct interviews in advance of upcoming hiring needs. When a candidate is selected, their profile is activated in our talent pool for this specialty area. 
We notify our talent pool candidates first whenever we have a project need.
