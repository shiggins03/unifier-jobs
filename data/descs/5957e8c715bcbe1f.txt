About the Role

Oracle’s Construction and Engineering Global Business Unit (CEGBU) provides enterprise project and portfolio management solutions, including Primavera Unifier, P6 EPPM, Oracle Primavera Cloud, and Aconex.

We are seeking a 
Technical Integration Lead with proven, hands-on experience designing and developing integrations and data migrations using REST APIs through integration tools or middleware
, such as Oracle Integration Cloud (OIC) or Project Integration Framework (PIF).

This role combines client-facing technical leadership with direct development responsibility. You will own delivery from requirements gathering and data mapping through solution design, development, unit testing, UAT support, and production deployment.

Direct integration development experience is essential. Experience limited to functional consulting, project coordination, or QA/testing on integration projects does not meet this requirement. Prior Oracle Primavera integration experience is an added advantage, but is not required.
