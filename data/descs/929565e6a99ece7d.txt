About the Positions

We’re seeking 
Quality Managers
 to support large, complex heavy civil infrastructure programs, including highways, roads, bridges, transit (bus and rail), aviation (international airports), and water/wastewater projects delivered through both traditional and alternative delivery methods. These positions run the quality assurance program. You’ll set the standard, audit whether committed quality control plans are actually being followed, and make sure nonconformances get closed instead of carried. Luster’s own quality management system is ISO 9001:2015 certified, so the framework you work inside is audited rather than sitting in a binder.

This post is for future roles and not for a specific opportunity that is available right now. We accept applications and conduct interviews in advance of upcoming hiring needs. When a candidate is selected, their profile is activated in our talent pool for this specialty area. 
We notify our talent pool candidates first whenever we have a project need.
