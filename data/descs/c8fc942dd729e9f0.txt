About the Position

We’re seeking a 
Utilities Coordinator
 to support a large, complex, heavy-civil rail infrastructure program. This role will be responsible for identifying utility conflicts within the project corridor and coordinating with public and private utility owners to resolve them before they affect design or construction, keeping utility clearance aligned with the program’s overall schedule.

This is a long-term, full-time, onsite position located in the greater San Francisco Bay Area, CA.

Responsibilities
 
may include, but are not limited to, the following:

Identify existing utilities and coordinate new installations, developing strategies to resolve conflicts before they affect design or construction.

Coordinate with public and private utility owners to confirm ownership, records, and relocation requirements, and negotiate utility agreements, cost responsibility, and reimbursement terms with project leadership and legal counsel.

Maintain the utility conflict matrix, driving each conflict to a documented clearance decision, and track relocation schedules against committed dates, escalating slippage before it reaches the critical path.

Prepare utility permit applications and confirm that utility coordination and design conform to accommodation policies, right of way requirements, and environmental regulations.

Review design plans, construction documents, and record drawings to identify conflicts and route them for resolution, and support construction teams during field investigations and relocations.

Coordinate subsurface utility engineering work and utility activities in dense urban corridors, including closure and outage windows.

Maintain detailed coordination records, correspondence, and approvals to support payment, audit, and claim review.
