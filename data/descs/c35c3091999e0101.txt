Position Brand Description:
The Global Facilities Delivery (GFD) Organization, part of Corporate Engineering, delivers new and renovated capital assets around the world.  Our mission is accomplished through a highly leveraged organization of design, construction and qualification contractors.
Many of the assets delivered focus on manufacturing medical innovations and delivering innovative solutions to patients.  Some of the assets delivered support an expansion in supply of existing medicines. 
Role Summary:
The CSA Capital Cost Estimator develops owner-side Class 5 through Class 1 estimates for civil, structural, architectural, site development, building envelope, and related construction scopes supporting complex pharmaceutical manufacturing and infrastructure projects. The role converts evolving engineering scope, design information, execution strategies, market data, and benchmarks into complete, traceable, and decision-ready estimates. The role will assess demolition, temporary works, weather impacts, site constraints, phasing, access, laydown, cranes, and other constructability-driven cost factors.
