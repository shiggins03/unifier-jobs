JOB POSITION OVERVIEW:
The Sr. Director, Central Estimating & Planning sits within the Capital Portfolio Operations (PortOps) team in Global Facilities Delivery (GFD), part of Manufacturing & Quality, and reports to the AVP, Capital Portfolio Operations. This role owns the capital estimate from initial concept through project closeout for GFD's capital portfolio of approximately $65 billion, and is accountable for establishing the estimating and planning strategy that delivers predictable and accurate cost outcomes across that portfolio. Scope includes basis of estimate, basis of schedule, estimate and schedule validation, contingency and allowance setting, and benchmarking. This role is accountable for developing the team’s structure, defining the hiring plan, hiring and onboarding estimators, and establishing the team’s operating processes for this function. The role works across a large number of senior stakeholders, including Network AVPs, program directors, Finance, and GPCO, and is expected to influence decisions through others where it does not hold direct authority. Estimating strategy is owned by this role and is aligned with the project controls framework and tools established by the Global Portfolio Controls Office (GPCO).
