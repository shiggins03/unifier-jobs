About the Position

We’re seeking an experienced 
Project Coordinator
 to support large, complex heavy civil infrastructure programs, including highways, roads, bridges, transit (bus and rail), aviation (international airports), and water/wastewater projects delivered through both traditional and alternative delivery methods. You’ll keep the administrative side of a major transportation program running smoothly, from travel and staffing actions to events, facilities needs, and leadership calendars. This position also serves as a go-to resource for the other project coordinators in the construction office, helping keep administrative practices consistent across teams.

This is a long-term, full-time, on-site position located in Fresno, CA.

Responsibilities
 
may include, but are not limited to, the following:

Prepare travel requests, arrange bookings and itinerary changes, and process travel expense claims and expense reports for program staff.

Track personnel requisitions and staffing actions from submission through approval, including candidate packages, interview scheduling, projected and actual start dates, and offboarding steps.

Coordinate meetings, conferences, and events, including room reservations, A/V, catering, and materials, and attend sessions to capture notes and action items when needed (occasional travel to meeting sites).

Support facilities and office logistics, and serve as a flexible administrative resource for program offices that need extra help.

Maintain Outlook calendars for leadership, scheduling meetings with a clear sense of what takes priority.

Develop and maintain presentations, correspondence, reports, and spreadsheets, and keep electronic and paper files organized for quick retrieval.

Perform quality checks on documents and correspondence so they follow required program formats before they go out.

Support change management tracking and keep change records current.
