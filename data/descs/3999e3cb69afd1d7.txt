STV is seeking a 
Digital Solutions Specialist
 to join our Digital Advisory department at our Boston, MA office. This role supports the delivery of digital solutions that help clients improve project execution, stakeholder collaboration, and data-driven decision-making. Working under the guidance of senior team members, the Digital Solutions Specialist will assist in implementing digital tools, developing efficient workflows, and supporting project and program teams with technology-enabled solutions.
The ideal candidate is passionate about leveraging technology to improve business processes, organizing and analyzing project information, and supporting clients through digital transformation initiatives. This position offers an excellent opportunity to gain experience across project delivery, digital systems implementation, and process improvement within complex infrastructure and construction programs.
Core Responsibilities
Support the implementation and maintenance of digital solutions that align with client project and governance requirements.
Assist with configuring, testing, and deploying project management and construction management platforms.
Collect, organize, and analyze project data to develop reports, dashboards, and actionable insights for project teams and stakeholders.
Support contract administration and project controls workflows, including document management, RFIs, submittals, change tracking, and project closeout activities.
Collaborate with project teams, clients, and internal stakeholders to identify opportunities for process improvements and increased efficiency.
Help develop and maintain digital workflows, standards, templates, and best practices.
Provide user support, training, and adoption assistance for digital tools and platforms.
Participate in continuous improvement initiatives focused on enhancing project delivery and operational performance.
Perform other duties as assigned in support of project and client objectives.
