JOB POSITION OVERVIEW:
The Network Performance & Assurance Lead sits within the Capital Portfolio Operations (PortOps) team in Global Facilities Delivery (GFD), part of Manufacturing & Quality, and reports to the AVP, Capital Portfolio Operations. This role supports an assigned network portfolio within GFD's capital delivery footprint (e.g., the API Dry Network, the Parenteral & Device Network, or another network as assigned by PortOps leadership) and is responsible for portfolio performance metrics, audit coordination, after-action reviews, and performance governance for that network's capital projects. The role coordinates GPCO controls standards, PMO resourcing, Operational Excellence & Governance processes, and Portfolio Comms support across the network's project teams. The role also confirms that projects in the assigned network are executing to GPCO-defined Project Controls standards, forecasts where Project Controls capacity is needed, conducts delivery assurance reviews, and reviews contractor and Managed Service Provider (MSP) cost data on the Owner's behalf. Network assignment is set by PortOps leadership and may change as GFD's network structure evolves.
