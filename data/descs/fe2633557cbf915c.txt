About the Positions

We’re seeking 
Cost Controls Managers
 to support large, complex heavy civil infrastructure programs, including highways, roads, bridges, transit (bus and rail), aviation (international airports), and water/wastewater projects delivered through both traditional and alternative delivery methods. You’ll hold the cost position on an assigned program: what the work was budgeted at, what it is forecast to cost now, and what the gap between those two numbers means for funding and delivery decisions. This bank spans managers running cost control on a single program through leaders running the cost function across a portfolio.

This post is for future roles and not for a specific opportunity that is available right now. We accept applications and conduct interviews in advance of upcoming hiring needs. When a candidate is selected, their profile is activated in our talent pool for this specialty area. 
We notify our talent pool candidates first whenever we have a project need.

Responsibilities
 
may include, but are not limited to, the following:

Develop and maintain the cost baseline for an assigned program, including budget structure, work breakdown and cost coding, and the change control workflow that keeps the baseline current.

Review payment applications, cost reports, and invoices against contract terms, verified progress, and the approved schedule of values, then recommend approval or adjustment.

Evaluate change order and claim pricing for cost reasonableness, and track the cost exposure of pending changes so the program sees the number before it is committed.
