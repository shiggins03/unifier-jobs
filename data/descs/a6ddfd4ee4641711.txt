About the Position

We’re seeking an experienced 
Interface Manager
 to support a large, complex, heavy-civil rail infrastructure program. This role is responsible for establishing, implementing, and managing a formal Interface Management Plan, including the governance, processes, and tools required to identify, track, and resolve all technical and organizational interfaces across engineering, procurement, and construction activities. This position operates within multi-contract programs involving significant discipline integration and high-risk interface environments (e.g., tunneling, major utility coordination, or systems integration), requiring coordination across contractors, stakeholders, and technical teams to ensure alignment, minimize risk, and support efficient project execution.

This is a long-term, full-time, hybrid position (three days per week in office) position located in the greater San Francisco Bay Area, CA.

Responsibilities
 
may include, but are not limited to, the following:

Develop, maintain, and implement the project interface management plan, including the interface register, interface control documents, and the process for identifying, assigning, and closing interface points.

Identify technical and organizational interfaces across engineering, procurement, and construction activities, and coordinate resolution across design contracts, construction packages, and disciplines.

Chair interface coordination meetings, track open interface items to closure, and escalate unresolved items with a recommended path forward.

Review design submittals and deliverables for interface conformance, including spatial coordination, structural envelope, equipment clearance, access, and maintainability, and verify that the design supports long-term operations and maintenance rather than the minimum equipment footprint.
