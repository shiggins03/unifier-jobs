About the Position

We’re seeking an experienced 
Document Control Systems Manager
 to support a large-scale rail transit program. You’ll help modernize enterprise document control, coordinating a new electronic document management system (EDMS) from requirements through deployment and writing the policies, procedures, and reports that keep the program aligned with state requirements long after go-live.

This is a long-term, full-time, hybrid (three to four days per week in office) position located in Sacramento, CA.

Responsibilities
 
may include, but are not limited to, the following:

Oversee the EDMS effort from planning and procurement support through implementation and ongoing refinement, outlining scope, schedule, staffing, and expected outcomes for leadership review.

Coordinate with internal offices, IT, project teams, and document control operations leadership to turn day-to-day needs into confirmed system requirements, from business and records needs to security, technical, and end-user requirements.

Support EDMS selection and configuration, including requirements and evaluation criteria, vendor coordination, and recommended approaches for metadata, classification, permissions, workflows, naming standards, integrations, and reporting.

Track rollout plans covering testing, legacy data review and migration, accessibility, security, user acceptance, cutover, and post-launch support, and escalate issues that affect service.

Write, update, and maintain document control policies and procedures that reflect California public agency requirements for which documents and records must be controlled, retained, and made available.

Develop and recommend a program-wide document control framework of governance, roles, standards, templates, and performance metrics, and keep approved materials current.

Partner closely with records management to align document control practices with retention schedules, classification, and public records requirements.
