About the Position

We’re seeking an 
Office Engineer
 to support large, complex heavy civil infrastructure programs, including highways, roads, and transit projects delivered through both traditional and alternative delivery methods. You’ll keep project documentation, quality and safety records, and contract correspondence organized, current, and audit-ready so the project team can track performance and make well-documented decisions across multiple task orders.

This is a long-term, full-time, on-site position located in the greater DMV Area.

Responsibilities
 
may include, but are not limited to, the following:

Maintain project documentation, including correspondence, submittals, RFIs, meeting minutes, and daily reports, in Procore and other project systems.

Track submittals and RFIs through review and response, coordinating with technical reviewers to keep turnaround within contract requirements.

Maintain quality control records, including inspection reports, test results, and nonconformance reports, and track corrective actions to closure.

Support safety program administration by tracking incident reports, safety observations, and corrective actions, and compiling safety documentation for project reporting.

Verify quantities and supporting documentation submitted with monthly progress payment applications.

Support change management by logging change requests, change notices, and change orders, and assembling backup documentation for evaluation.

Prepare weekly and monthly status reports, logs, and dashboards tracking submittal, RFI, quality, and safety metrics.

Coordinate document control across multiple task orders, keeping files organized, current, and ready for audit.

Coordinate with project managers, resident engineers, inspectors, and contractor staff on document flow and contract compliance.

Support closeout by tracking as-built drawings, warranties, operations and maintenance manuals, and punch list documentation.
