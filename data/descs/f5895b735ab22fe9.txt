Position Brand Description
The Global Facilities Delivery (GFD) Organization, part of Corporate Engineering, delivers new and renovated capital assets around the world.  Our mission is accomplished through a highly leveraged organization of design, construction and qualification contractors.
Many of the assets delivered focus on manufacturing of medical innovations and delivering innovative solutions to patients.  Some of the assets delivered support an expansion in supply of existing medicines. 
Role Summary
The Advisor, Project Information Management & Document Control serves as the enterprise process owner for project information management, document control, and information governance within Global Facilities Delivery (GFD). The role establishes and governs the standards, processes, systems, and digital delivery requirements used to manage project information across the capital project lifecycle. The Advisor provides strategic leadership for information governance, project information systems, and digital delivery practices, ensuring information quality, consistency, traceability, compliance, and operational readiness across the global portfolio while driving continuous improvement and capability development.
